# Contents

## Chapter 1 — The Big Question
What is intelligence, and how does it begin? We start with the main idea behind this project and explain the key words we’ll use along the way.

## Chapter 2 — Life Learns and Changes
From simple living things to animals and humans, we explore how life takes in information, learns, adapts, and passes things on. We also look at evolution’s limits and the times things don’t work out.

## Chapter 3 — Thinking Beyond the Brain
People use language, books, tools, computers, and teamwork to do things no one person could do alone. Then we ask how AI fits into that story—and what it can really help us achieve.

## Chapter 4 — The Systems We Depend On
Our world runs on power, computers, and networks. We look at examples where these systems help us, where they fail, and how better planning can make them more reliable. We also ask who benefits and who pays the cost.

## Chapter 5 — What Does the Evidence Tell Us?
We bring the examples together, compare what they show, and separate facts from guesses. We ask what the evidence supports, what is still uncertain, and what we should investigate next.

---

*This is a starting contents page for the Intelligence Seed 001 research project. The chapters may change as the evidence develops. The goal is not to prove the main idea at any cost, but to find out which parts hold up when we test them.*
