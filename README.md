## What it proposes

The seed explores four provisional hypotheses: that new information-processing capabilities can emerge through physical or organizational bridges; that human civilization externalizes cognition into non-biological systems; that AI may help design subsequent cognitive infrastructure; and that resilient shared infrastructure could create practical common interests without requiring identical human and AI values.

It does **not** assume that evolution has a purpose, that AI is conscious, that AI is destined to replace humanity, or that human and AI values must converge.

## Independent evaluation is invited

Disagreement is welcome. Please test definitions, identify hidden assumptions, supply evidence and counter-evidence, propose falsifiable predictions, and develop alternative explanations. Agreement is not required.

Released versions should be treated as immutable. Submit critiques or extensions as separate responses, and preserve the parent version and hash when proposing a descendant.
